# Changelog

## 1.1.0

**New: where it hurts**
- A body map in Symptoms and pain. Click areas on the front or back of the body to mark where it hurts, and click again to say it's worse: mild, moderate or severe
- Insights shows a body heatmap of where it hurt most over any stretch of time, with the worst areas listed
- Printouts for your doctor start with the same heatmap, and every day lists where it hurt and how badly
- Search finds days by body area, like "knee" or "lower back", and exports include it too

## 1.0.1

**Simpler password setup**
- Setting a password now takes far fewer clicks: choose your password, save your recovery file, and one message confirms it's all done
- Removed the extra question about copying the recovery key
- Fixed a message about "older backups made before encryption" appearing for brand new diaries. Kaydence now quietly tidies away old backups that hold no diary days, and only asks about ones that really contain your writing
- The lock screen's "Forgot your password?" goes straight to choosing your recovery file

## 1.0.0, first public release

Kaydence is ready for everyone. Here's what it does:

**Writing**
- Opens straight onto today with the cursor ready, and saves everything by itself
- A freeform page: click anywhere to type, and move and resize anything
- Bold, italic, underline, text sizes, titles, headings, bullet and numbered lists, text colours and highlights
- Sticky notes in five colours, and checklists right on the page
- Photos by pasting, dragging in or picking files, with rotate and crop
- Drawing with pens, highlighters and shapes, with an eraser and lasso
- Voice notes of up to five minutes, played back on the page
- Undo and redo for everything on the page, and zoom

**Check-in**
- A daily check-in beside the page: feelings, note, sleep, fitness, tasks, struggles, wins, and water, caffeine, smoking and alcohol if you want them
- Arrange the sections in any order by right clicking them or in Settings, and hide the ones you don't need
- Optional health tools (medications, symptoms and pain, blood pressure, weight), a period and cycle tracker, and a transition section, all chosen in the welcome tour
- Days since counters in the sidebar, which count down for future dates

**Looking back**
- A calendar with mood colours, milestones and period marks
- Week, Month, Memories and Search screens
- Insights: mood over time, by weekday, month and season, your year in pixels, and health and cycle charts
- Print or save as PDF for any dates, with bold headings, drawings, and a doctor friendly option

**Keeping it safe**
- An optional password that encrypts every file in your diary with AES-256
- A recovery file as a spare key, saved straight after you set a password
- Daily backups, a backup folder of your choice, and one click restore
- Export everything to a web page, text files, photos and voice notes, or a single PDF
- Photos are cleaned when you add them, leaving behind hidden location and camera details, and turned the right way up

**Everything else**
- Light and dark mode, accent colours, page styles, and dyslexia friendly fonts and spacing
- Starts with Windows and waits by the clock if you like, with a shortcut to open it from anywhere
- A daily reminder to write
- A full guide built in, opened with F1
- Make a bug report in one click, with logs that never contain anything from your diary
- Checks once a day for a new version, which is the only thing it ever sends over the internet

## Before 1.0

Kaydence was built and tested privately through versions 0.1 to 0.5 before this first public release.
