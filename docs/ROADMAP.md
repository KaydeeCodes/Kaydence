# Roadmap

Ideas being looked at for future versions of Kaydence. Nothing here is promised, and the order may change. Got an idea of your own? Please [suggest a feature](https://github.com/KaydeeCodes/Kaydence/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

## Being looked at

- Using one diary on two PCs, for example through a OneDrive folder
- Choosing where your diary is stored
- Writing prompts for blank days
- Tags for entries, and searching by tag
- Private days, left out of printouts and search
- Your own custom check-in sections and trackers
- Playback speed for voice notes
- Smaller voice notes and an option to shrink very large photos
- Reminders to take medication

## Later, maybe

- Importing from other diary apps
- Translations into other languages
- Stickers for the page
